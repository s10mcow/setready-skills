# How SetReady reports look

Use this when you make a report's PDF with your own tools. The layout scripts, when a skill
has them, already follow it.

- **Page:** US Letter, portrait, 0.75 inch side margins, one column. A plain sans-serif such
  as Helvetica or Arial. Body text near-black (#171717 for headings, #4d4d4d for text),
  labels and small print grey (#888888), rules and dividers very light grey (#ebebeb).
- **Top of every page:** "SETREADY" in small spaced capitals on the left and a thin rule
  under it. From page 2, the report title and address in small grey type on the right.
- **Title block:** the title large and bold, the address line under it in grey, then a
  light grey box (#fafafa) holding the details as small grey capital labels over bold
  values, side by side.
- **Count tiles:** three tiles in a row under the title block, each a big number over a small
  capital label, on a pale tint with a 3 pt bar down its left side: red (#c62828 on
  #fdf3f3) for red flags, amber (#b45309 on #fff8ec) for open questions, green (#15803d on
  #f1f9f3) or near-black (#171717 on #fafafa) for the third.
- **Section headings:** bold, about 12.5 pt, with the item count in small grey after them.
  Never leave a heading alone at the bottom of a page.
- **Red flags:** one card each on pale red (#fdf3f3) with a 3 pt red bar down the left. The
  title bold; the detail under it; then two small columns, "Evidence" and "Who can confirm
  or clear it", each a small grey capital label over the text. A qualifier such as
  "Unverified" sits top right in amber.
- **Tables:** a light grey header row in small grey capitals with a darker rule under it,
  thin light rules between rows, no vertical lines, text aligned to the top of each cell.
  Money right-aligned. A total row in bold with a dark rule above it. Repeat the header row
  when a table runs onto a new page.
- **Status words:** colour the word itself, bold: Verified and Settled green, Unverified and
  Open amber, Red flag red. Anything after the word stays plain.
- **Settled list:** a green tick before each line, thin rules between lines.
- **Next step:** a light grey box with a 3 pt near-black bar down the left. Links blue
  (#0070f3) and clickable.
- **Closing:** a thin rule, then the "Made with" line and its link in small grey type,
  kept on the same page as the last section.
- **Bottom of every page:** a thin rule, the disclaimer in small grey type on the left and
  "Page x of y" on the right.

# Evidence status

Every fact about a parcel, a home or a plan gets one of three statuses. Show the status
next to the fact, every time.

| Status | Means | Write it as |
|---|---|---|
| **Verified** | You read it in a source you can name, and you can give the date you read it. | "Verified: flood zone X, FEMA flood map, read 2026-11-17." |
| **Unverified** | Someone said it, a listing claims it, or you inferred it, and no source confirms it yet. | "Unverified: seller says the lot perks." |
| **Red flag** | A source shows something that can stop the project or change its cost a lot, or two sources disagree. | "Red flag: mapped wetlands cover the back third of the lot (NWI, read 2026-11-17). Confirm with a soil scientist." |

Rules:

1. A person's say-so is unverified until a document or an official source confirms it,
   even when that person is the seller, the agent or the user.
2. Desk research never makes a parcel "buildable". Never say "buildable: yes". Say which
   checks passed, which are unverified, and which are red flags.
3. Every red flag names the kind of pro or office that can confirm or clear it. Use the
   names in references/pro-roles.md.
4. When two sources disagree, report both, with dates, and mark it a red flag.
5. Data goes out of date. A verified fact more than a year old is unverified again.

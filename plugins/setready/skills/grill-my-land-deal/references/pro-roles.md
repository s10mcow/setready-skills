# Kinds of pro on a land-home job

Use these names when you tell someone who can answer a question or confirm a finding. The
second column is the role to pass to SetReady's `search_pros` (as `category`, trades too) or
`assemble_crew` (in `roles`) when the person's agent is connected to SetReady.

| Kind of pro | SetReady role | Reach for them when |
|---|---|---|
| Real estate agent | `land` | Finding or buying the parcel, comps, offer terms |
| Surveyor | `surveyor` | Boundaries, where easements and access roads sit on the ground, setbacks, a boundary or site survey |
| Soil scientist | `soil_scientist` | Septic suitability, soil evaluation or perc test, wetland lines |
| Septic | `septic` | Septic design, permit and install |
| Well drilling | `well` | A private well where there is no public water |
| Site / land prep | `site_gc` | Clearing, fill, pad, driveway, culvert, grading; often the site general contractor |
| Foundation engineer | `foundation_engineer` | Foundation design or the engineer's letter a loan may require |
| Home dealer | `dealer` | Choosing, pricing and ordering the home |
| Manufacturer | `manufacturer` | Floor plans, options and specs straight from the plant |
| Installer | `installer` | Delivering, setting, leveling and tying down the home |
| Electrician | `electrician` | Service pole or meter base, hookup from the utility to the home |
| HVAC | `hvac` | Heating and cooling hookup |
| Trade contractor | `trade_sub` | Trades not listed here, such as plumbing hookups |
| Skirting | `skirting` | Skirting around the base of the home |
| Decks | `decks` | Decks, porches and steps |
| Lender | `lender` | Land loans, land-home loans, chattel loans, construction-to-permanent |
| Title services | `title` | Title search and insurance, legal access and recorded easements, closing, retiring a home's title to real property |

Some questions belong to an office, not a pro. Name the office instead:

- **County planning or zoning office:** whether a manufactured home is allowed, minimum size, setbacks.
- **County building department:** building, setup and electrical permits, inspections, the certificate of occupancy (CO).
- **County or state health or environmental agency:** septic and well permits, depending on the state.
- **County property appraiser or assessor, and the clerk or register of deeds:** owner of record, recorded deeds, easements and restrictions.
- **The power company:** cost and time to bring service to the home site.

Coverage: SetReady lists pros in Florida, Georgia, Alabama, South Carolina, North Carolina,
Tennessee and Mississippi. Elsewhere, name the kind of pro and say SetReady doesn't cover
that county yet. A listing is a directory entry, not an endorsement, and a listed license
number is not a current verification: check it with the state board before hiring.
